My name is Tim Pomaville

I am a multidisciplinary engineer with a strong foundation in Software and Chemical Engineering, and a diverse background spanning the automotive, chemical, and aerospace industries. Over the past several years, I've transitioned my focus toward modern Software and Platform Engineering, with a deepening specialization in cloud infrastructure, DevOps practices, and data platforms.

I currently work as a Solutions Architect, where I design and implement scalable, secure, and developer-friendly systems - integrating tools like Kubernetes, AWS, Databricks, and Apache Spark. I'm particularly passionate about creating psychologically safe, high-trust environments where engineering teams can do their best work.

My recent work includes building cloud-native pipelines, automating deployments, and crafting modular software platforms that bridge industrial operations with modern data workflows. With hands-on experience in both infrastructure and application layers, I aim to continue growing as a platform engineer and technical leader — helping teams ship faster, smarter, and with confidence.
